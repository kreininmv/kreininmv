I'm a member of technical staff at [xAID](https://xaid.ai). I build AI for CT and MRI diagnostics and take it from research ideas to models that radiologists use every day.

### Work

- [**CT pathology foundation model**](https://nebius.com/customer-stories/xaid): SOTA on [CT-RATE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ibrahimhamamci/CT-RATE) with mean AUC `0.901`. In production it catches `40%` of the findings radiologists miss.
- [**Medical Flow Matching**](https://github.com/intsystems/Kreinin-MS-Thesis): translates between contrast-enhanced and non-contrast CT. Its synthetic data adds `+14%` segmentation IoU across modules.
- **Agentic labeling harness**: VLM agents that produce soft labels when expert annotation is scarce.
- **Production performance**: custom CUDA kernels and pipeline optimizations made production pipelines `2×` faster, and a C++ DICOM loader reads studies `2.4×` faster.
- **Clinical modules**, with scores from partner clinics' evaluations: Aortic aneurysm and dissection, Chest CT nine lung pathologies, Pleural effusion, Coronary artery calcium scoring, Pulmonary emphysema, Lumbosacral spine MRI, Pancreatitis classification, Pneumoperitoneum detection.


### Projects

- [**RSNA 2026 Knee Abnormality Detection**](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection): kaggle silver medal, 60th of 6,425 (top 1%), solo
- [**Implicit Reparameterization Trick**](https://github.com/intsystems/implicit-reparameterization-trick): a PyTorch implementation of [Figurnov et al., NeurIPS 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08498) for the Gamma, Beta, Dirichlet, Student's t and von Mises distributions
- [**Kalman Filter and Extensions**](https://github.com/intsystems/Kalman-filter-and-his-friends): trainable Kalman filters in PyTorch, including linear, extended, unscented, variational Bayes and deep KF
- [**Medical Viewer**](https://github.com/kreininmv/Medical-viewer): an interactive CT and MRI viewer for Jupyter with mask overlays that reads DICOM, NIfTI and NRRD
- [**A programming language**](https://github.com/kreininmv/1-sem_MIPT/tree/main/T07_LANG): a C-like language with a recursive-descent parser, symbolic differentiation and a backend that targets a self-written CPU emulator
- [**Ray tracer and animation system**](https://github.com/kreininmv/PML-30): 3D graphics in C++, written in school
